What the site is

My Grades is a free, unofficial tool that reads grades from Skyward through ClassLink and shows them with what-if scores, a "what do I need?" calculator and a calculator for the ranking and cumulative GPA. It's a personal project. It isn't made by, endorsed by or affiliated with Skyward, ClassLink or any school or school district.

  • Don't rely on this site for any important decision. Check with Skyward, your teacher or your counselor.
